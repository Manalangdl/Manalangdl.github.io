Discover Columbia's Top Foods

Welcome to ColaMunchies, where we bring you the best places to eat in and around Columbia, SC. Whether you are a student, local, tourist, or simply a foodie, we have you covered on all types of taste buds. Our mission is to show the different cultures and help ease the decision making.

Lindsay Manalang

Cola Munchies is a website dedicated to satisfying your cravings and curiosity. My interest in food blogging stemmed from my parents’ love language in food. They are the ones that taught me the importance of food not just for nutrition but as a way to bond with family and friends.

I am originally from Myrtle Beach, SC and now I attend the University of South Carolina, I realized I miss a lot of foods from home. I was used to my parents cooking Filipino food all the time but now I am mainly cook on my own. However, I am very open to trying new foods in Columbia because of my love for cultures.

We are dedicated to exploring various cuisines, finding local sit-down restaurants, quick bites, and overall food events.
