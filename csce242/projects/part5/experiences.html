Food Experiences

Columbia, SC makes it easy to discover new food because every corner of the city has its own flavor and personality. Trying different spots becomes its own kind of adventure, whether it’s a hidden Soda City or a the state fair.

Featured Food Experiences

Big Flavors. Local Events.

*Saturdays

9am - 1pm

Soda City Market

Main Street, Columbia SC

Shop, snack, and explore Columbia’s favorite Saturday tradition. Browse local vendors, grab food from rotating food trucks and makers, and enjoy live entertainment along Main Street.

*Please note the dates may vary

Annually

*Oct 15 - 25

State Fair

1200 Rosewood Dr, Columbia SC

Classic fair food, thrilling rides, and plenty of Carolina tradition. Spend the day enjoying everything from funnel cakes and fried favorites to live entertainment, exhibits, and carnival rides.

*Please note the dates may vary

*November 19

6pm - 10pm

Vista Lights

The Vista, Columbia SC

Kick off the holiday season with food, lights, music, and local shopping. Stroll through the Vista, enjoy festive entertainment and the tree lighting, then stop into neighborhood restaurants and shops.

*Please note the dates may vary

Annually

*April 17 - 18

Columbia International Festival

1200 Rosewood Dr, Columbia SC

Taste your way around the world without leaving Columbia. Explore international cuisines, cultural performances, traditional arts, and vendors representing communities from around the globe.

*Please note the dates may vary
